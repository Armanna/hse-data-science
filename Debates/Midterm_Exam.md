Midterm/Exam Procedure (How to Argue in English)
Choose one of the six available exam groups here no later than Monday, March 16, 18:00.
After registration closes, instructors will randomly divide each group into subgroups of 6 students. Each subgroup will be assigned a specific start time for the exam (for example, 13:00, 14:00, and 15:00 for the afternoon slots, one hour per group). This time cannot be changed and students cannot swap slots with each other.
At the beginning of your exam slot, your subgroup will be randomly assigned one debate topic from the list of the topics covered during the course. 
Each subgroup will have 5 minutes to prepare.
After preparation, the instructor will randomly assign 3 students to the Proposition side (“For”) and 3 students to the Opposition side (“Against”).
Each student will have 7-9 minutes to deliver their speech.
There will be no audience (and hence no Q&A session), only the 6 debaters and the instructor.
The assessment criteria are the same as for normal debates, see the document, with two exceptions:
There will be no Q&A session, so there is no requirement to answer questions.
References to the model debates need not be explicit, i.e. you do not have to recall the names of the panelists from the model debate, you can merely say, e.g., “the panelist who was arguing against/for the motion”. (Remember that the first speaker has to make 4 references to the model debate, other speakers are required to make 2 references to the model debate and 2 – to other participants.)
The session will be recorded in case of grading disputes/appeals.
We will try to ensure that no student receives a debate topic on which they previously served as a panelist during the course.
